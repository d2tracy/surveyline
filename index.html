Survey Method & Coverage
Survey placement

Use the existing evenly spaced, centered survey line placement.

Tests different starting offsets to find the lowest calculated survey time for your line spacing and orientation while checking simplified swath coverage; this does not optimize vessel routing.

For manual spacing, the optimization assumes a usable swath equal to the specified line spacing; actual seafloor coverage must be verified.

Requires a bathymetry TIFF marked Bathymetry, and Calculate from swath → Calculate from depth + beam angles with valid depth, angles and overlap.

Models the port and starboard swaths from actual raster depth sampled along each planned line. Exports an 8-bit, single-band classified TIFF: red = no coverage, yellow = one line, green = two or more lines. NoData is transparent.

Calculate Depth from Bathymetry

Calculate the mean of valid bathymetry pixel values inside your selected survey-area polygon. This mean value is calculated from a bathymetry TIFF layer that you have added to the project in the Layers panel. Only pixel centres inside the polygon count; NoData and polygon holes are excluded. The raster must be marked Bathymetry in Layers.

This is a pixel-centre mean, not an area-weighted or vertical-datum-corrected sounding.

Orientation Finder

The selected survey area is analysed as a 3 × 3 grid. Each arrow shows the mean downhill slope direction estimated from bathymetry pixels in that zone; grid boundaries are hidden.

The arrows indicate downhill aspect, not the proposed survey-line direction. For contour-parallel lines, orientation is roughly 90° from the arrows. Assumes negative raster values are elevations below datum and positive values represent depth (positive down). NoData and flat cells are excluded.

Estimated survey time

Estimated time = line distance ÷ (speed × 1,852 m/h per knot) + number of turns × turn time. One turn is assumed between consecutive lines; extra transit, deployment and recovery are excluded.

Export Survey Lines

Export survey lines as GPX/KML, save the visible map as a georeferenced TIFF, or download a survey-plan summary PDF.

Only lines remaining in your plan are exported, including lines that are currently hidden. Coordinates use WGS84.

Save As… lets you choose a folder and file name in supported browsers (including Chrome on desktop). Download uses your browser's normal download location or prompt. GeoTIFF captures the visible map content (not the controls or floating legend) in EPSG:3857 and exports it at a higher resolution for sharper vector outlines.
