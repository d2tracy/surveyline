Calculate Depth from Bathymetry

Calculate the mean of valid bathymetry pixel values inside your selected survey-area polygon. This mean value is calculated from a bathymetry TIFF layer that you have added to the project in the Layers panel. Only pixel centres inside the polygon count; NoData and polygon holes are excluded. The raster must be marked Bathymetry in Layers.

This is a pixel-centre mean, not an area-weighted or vertical-datum-corrected sounding.

Calculate Projected Survey Coverage

Predicts seabed coverage along your existing survey lines using actual depths from a GeoTIFF marked Bathymetry. At each position along a line, beam angles and local depth determine the predicted port and starboard swath widths.

Beam angles are measured outward from vertical. Angles saved with your plan are filled in automatically; otherwise, enter them here.

Red: no coverage Yellow: one survey line Green: overlap (2+ lines)

Only pixels inside the original survey polygon with valid bathymetry are classified. The result is a georeferenced, single-band, colour-palette GeoTIFF added to Projected Survey Coverages. Download it using the ↓ TIFF button in that section.
